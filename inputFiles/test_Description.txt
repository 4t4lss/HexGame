Test0:
Perfomes a basic game on a 7x7 board. This test does not test edge cases. It only tests normal gameplay with no mistakes, including claiming hexes, switching players, and winning.

test1: 
Tests some edge cases. Uses an 11x11 board.
- (ln3) Claiming a hex out of range of the board
- (ln6) Player attempting to claim on their opponents turn
- Ending with no win
    - Ends before anyone has won
    - Tests a line of hexes with one empty
    - Tests a line of hexes with one enemy hex
